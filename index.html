Sindhu Shakha

Shakha activities and highlights:

  • In January, Balas started their first Bal-Shakha. The Balas managed all activities themselves, including Yoga, Khel, Baudhik, and Prarthana.
  • The highlight of the Shakha was the Baudhik presented by Anurag Shahi, a 9-year-old Bal, on the topic “Importance of Khel in Shakha.”
  • Throughout the month, Balas actively participated in SNY activities, and a few of them were awarded the SNY medal.
  • Celebrated Basant Panchami Utsav!
  • Our talented young Bala and Kishore members showcased their incredible skills, making every moment truly unforgettable.
